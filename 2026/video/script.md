# Four places at the table — narrated walkthrough

**Design proposal · Ending A: ambiguous “hers” · ENDING STILL UNDECIDED.**
Based on captain notes and live storyboard revision 06, read 2026-09-30. This is an abridged representative run, not a fixed sequence of failures or prescribed survivor count. All dialogue, player names, individual choices, visual staging and exact fictional death details are firstmate/scout proposals. None is a claim about a historical family.

## What this run demonstrates
Four guests enter: Ada, Ben, Cora (healer), Dax (secret Tory spy). An honest tutorial draw blinds Ada. Five later challenge outcomes and their chosen payments leave Ada and Cora at the finale. Ada is never protected against sacrifice. Two survivors is an illustrated outcome, not an imposed rule. Other runs may reach the ending with 1–4 under the storyboard draft. “Survivor” means reached the finale; all four are claimed in ending A.

The cost menu remains offering / eligible curse / person. This particular group keeps its four offering tokens throughout; the film deliberately shows their mistaken attachment to the promised prize, not a rule requiring human payment. No extra random sacrifice table is invented. Honest card chance, play and voluntary payments determine this run. This is not evidence that the economy is balanced; easy token payments may make removals rare.

Healer: one non-Veiled cure, used on Ben's hearing. Additional curses: deafened Ben (then cured), silenced Cora, no-hands Ben. No actual hand bindings. Blindness lasts through Ada's spoken ending. The silence curse permits pointing and tapping. Mediums are innocent. The spy offers only one wrong suggestion, never obstructs physically. Keepsakes/seals stay separate from spendable offerings and stay with the group after removals.

The card test uses five equally likely symbols with replacement and reshuffling after every draw. Five correct guesses in one streak: (1/5)^5 = 1/3,125. A lucky win pays nothing. Failures shown in later scenes are this sample group's submitted wrong answers, not secretly rigged outcomes.

## Production frame
Later spiritualist parlor recalling the Revolutionary-era home. Indoor conceptual stations only; no measured room or exit layout. Costumes, audience intensity and room mapping remain deferred. No Ouija build. Fog, knocks, singing-glass-like tones, slate and silhouette are candidate effects, not selected purchases. Private sacrifice epilogues are compressed in this film. Two performers must rehearse staffed handoffs without leaving Ada to navigate alone.

Voices: local Kokoro narrator `bm_george`, ghost mother `af_nicole`, medium `bf_emma`; four finale echoes use a locally processed `af_sky` voice. No external voice service or licensed music.

## 02. The card chooses

**Narrator:** Two reached this ending; neither escaped the fictional bargain. Did they become her children, or something she owns? That ending is still the captain’s choice.
