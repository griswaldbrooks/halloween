# Halloween 2026 game: knowns and unknowns ledger

Scout report, 2026-10-08. Task `hw-unknowns-ledger`. Notes only. Nothing in the halloween repo, the storyboard, or the notes was changed. No Lavish session was opened.

This is an inventory of what is decided, what is proposed, and what is open for the Halloween 2026 séance game, organized by stage, with the source for each item and who owns each open one. It does not propose answers or puzzles.

## How to read it

Status:
- **Decided**: the captain said it, or the 2026-10-07 handoff records it as settled.
- **Proposed**: written down by a designer or the handoff as a direction, not approved.
- **Open**: nobody has decided it, or the current source says it is unresolved.

Owner of an open item:
- **C**: captain call. Taste, story, what the mother intends, cost, how dark the content gets.
- **D**: designer proposal. Puzzle mechanics, prop form, timing. The captain only approves.
- **C/D**: the captain sets the direction, the designer works out the form.

Sources, in order of precedence. A later source overrides an earlier one where they conflict:
1. **V-10-08**: the captain's voice, 2026-10-08, as relayed in this task's brief. Hub and spoke; first half structurally known; puzzles need teasing out; after the cursing is unknown.
